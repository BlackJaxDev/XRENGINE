# Commercial and Private-Engine Licensing

XRENGINE licensing beyond the Community Source License is available only
through agreements signed by the applicable XRENGINE Licensor and the
customer. BlackJax represents the Licensor and handles licensing requests.

There is no public order form, click-through license, automatic purchase,
published price, or published royalty schedule. Contact
[blackjax0@gmail.com](mailto:blackjax0@gmail.com) to request terms.

## Separate Agreements

### Commercial License

A Commercial License permits the named application to be monetized within its
signed scope. Engine modifications must remain public unless the customer also
obtains a separate Private Engine Modification License.

Request a Commercial License before an XRENGINE application:

- is sold, rented, subscribed to, or requires paid access;
- contains purchases, paid servers, passes, downloadable content, digital
  items, virtual currency, marketplaces, or other transactions;
- contains advertising, sponsorships, or affiliate promotions;
- receives application-related donations, tips, crowdfunding, commissions,
  fees, or revenue shares; or
- is delivered through paid development, publishing, licensing,
  distribution, or work-for-hire.

### Private Engine Modification License

A Private Engine Modification License is a separate contract permitting
specified engine changes to remain non-public for specified products,
deployments, and entities.

It must be requested expressly before private engine changes are distributed
or externally deployed, including on a server. It does not itself permit
monetization.

A customer that wants both monetization and private engine changes must request
and sign both agreements.

See [LICENSE.md](../LICENSE.md) for the controlling definitions and public
license requirements.

## Indie

Indie licensing is intended for independent developers and small studios with
limited organizational, product, deployment, and support needs. Indie terms
are intended to remain accessible and proportionate to the project.

Eligibility and all financial, reporting, scope, and duration terms are
determined individually during contact. No public threshold or quoted example
creates eligibility or grants a license.

## Enterprise

Enterprise licensing is intended for organizations or uses involving broader
scope or risk, including:

- multiple products, affiliates, publishers, or studios;
- shared private engine forks;
- hosted platforms, software development kits, white-label products, or
  high-scale deployment;
- source escrow, console, security, certification, or regulatory requirements;
- support, warranties, service commitments, or custom engineering; and
- custom procurement, liability, assignment, or intellectual-property terms.

Enterprise classification and all contract terms are negotiated individually.
